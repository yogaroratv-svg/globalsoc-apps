What the audit looks at

📧 Email spoofing

SPF, DMARC and DKIM decide whether criminals can send email that looks like it came from you. We check that each exists, is valid, and actually enforces.

🔏 Integrity & certificates

DNSSEC stops forged DNS answers. CAA limits which certificate authorities can issue HTTPS certificates for your domain.

🧩 Takeover & exposure

Dangling CNAMEs, dead name servers, internal IPs, wildcards and leftover verification records are common footholds for attackers.

🧭 Resilience

Name server count, network diversity and SOA timers determine whether your domain stays up when one server or provider fails.

🏷️ Registration

An expiring domain or a missing transfer lock can lose you your website and email overnight. We read public RDAP data where the registry publishes it.

🛰️ Server-side tests

Our Azure backend checks your HTTPS certificate, outdated TLS, security headers, HSTS, security.txt and your MTA-STS policy file, and asks each name server directly about zone transfers, open recursion and version disclosure. These are light, standard checks, not a penetration test.
