FAQ

Common questions

Is there a minimum order?

Ten boxes. Volume discounts start at 25 boxes (5%) and go up to 20% at 500 boxes.

How do gift links work?

Each recipient gets an email with a private link. They choose one box from the set you approve and enter their own delivery address, so you never handle home addresses. You pay up front for every link; any links not redeemed within 60 days are credited back.

Is our employees' data safe?

Recipient lists are used only to fulfil your order and are deleted 90 days after the last delivery. In this demo, everything you type stays in your own browser and is never uploaded.

Are the prices with or without GST?

Box prices are shown excluding GST, the way most businesses budget. The quote shows GST separately and the total includes it. You get a tax invoice for every order.

Can we include our own merchandise?

Yes. Send us branded items (drink bottles, notebooks, apparel) and we'll pack one in each box for $3 per box.

What about dietary needs and alcohol?

Every food box has a vegan and gluten-free version. Boxes with wine are only delivered to adults aged 18+ with a signature, and are not sent to recipients who opt out.
