In short
  • We only collect what you choose to give us: a message through a form, or an email address to get DNS monitoring alerts.

  • Email yogesh@globalsoc.com.au to see, correct or delete anything we hold about you.

Who we are

Global SOC Pty Ltd is a small app design and security studio based in Glen Waverley, Melbourne, Victoria. In this policy, "we" and "us" means Global SOC Pty Ltd. We aim to handle personal information in line with the Australian Privacy Principles in the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth).

Information stored only in your browser

Some tools remember things on your own device using your browser's local storage, for example your recent DNS audits, your Home Security self-check answers, and the demo apps' settings. This stays on your device; we can't see it. You can clear it at any time in your browser settings.

Services we use

We rely on these providers to run the site. They may process your information, including your IP address, on our behalf or as part of delivering the page to you:

  • GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc., USA) hosts the website.
  • Cloudflare (Cloudflare, Inc.) provides our DNS and routes email to us.
  • FormSubmit (formsubmit.co) receives contact and booking form submissions and forwards them to our inbox.
  • Microsoft Azure: Azure Functions runs DNS Audit's server-side checks in the Australia East region; Azure Table Storage holds monitoring subscriptions in Australia East; Azure Communication Services sends the monitoring emails, with its data location set to Australia.
  • Google Fonts (Google LLC) serves the fonts on our pages.
  • DNS Audit lookups: when you run an audit, your browser also queries the public services Google Public DNS (dns.google), Cloudflare DNS (cloudflare-dns.com), rdap.org and crt.sh directly.
  • IP geolocation: to describe the approximate location and network provider of a visitor's IP address, our backend looks it up with the public services ipwho.is and ip-api.com.

Some of these providers are based in, or store data in, other countries, including the United States. We choose providers that have strong security practices.

Email we send

We only send DNS monitoring emails to people who asked for them and confirmed by clicking the link in our confirmation email. Every email identifies us and has a one-click unsubscribe link, consistent with the Spam Act 2003 (Cth). We don't add you to any other mailing list.

Security

All traffic to this site and our backend is encrypted with HTTPS. Monitoring data is stored in Microsoft Azure with access limited to us. No system is perfectly secure, but if we ever have a data breach likely to cause you serious harm, we'll tell you and, where required, the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner (OAIC).

Access, correction and deletion

You can ask what personal information we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it, by emailing yogesh@globalsoc.com.au. We'll respond within 30 days.

Complaints

If you're unhappy with how we've handled your information, contact us first at the address above and we'll try to fix it. If you're still not satisfied, you can contact the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner.

Changes

We'll update this page if how we handle information changes, and change the date at the top.
