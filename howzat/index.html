Howzat

Cricket tipping and party picks with mates, pub leagues and sponsor prizes. Nobody stakes money, so there are no gambling licences, AUSTRAC or betting taxes.

How it makes money

  • SponsorsBrands fund the weekly and season prizes and pay for the placement.
  • PubsVenues pay about $49/month to run a pub league that brings punters in on game night.
  • Pro$4.99/month for form stats, reminders and private-league extras.
  • LaterAffiliate or licensed partners, once there is an audience and legal advice.
Demo. The fixture, results, players, pubs, sponsors and prizes are made up. BBL team names and colours are used only to show the format, and nothing here is endorsed by any team, Cricket Australia or the BBL.
