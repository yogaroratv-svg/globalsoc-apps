Kneadly

Clickable iOS prototype: find massage deals around Melbourne by time slot, book, pay in-app or at the venue, and show the voucher at reception.

Try it: pick a time slot → open a venue → choose a deal and treatment → pay with Apple Pay, card, PayPal, Afterpay or at the venue.

Demo only. Every venue, discount and price here is fictional. Venues sit in real Melbourne suburbs, but their names don't belong to real businesses. No payment is taken.
