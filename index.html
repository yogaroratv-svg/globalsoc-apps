FAQ

Common questions

How much does an app cost?

A clickable prototype is a small fixed price. A full build depends on features like logins, payments and admin tools. After the idea call we give you a fixed quote for each stage, with no surprises.

Do I need a technical background?

No. Bring the idea and your knowledge of your customers. We handle design, technology, the App Store and hosting, and explain the choices in plain English.

Who owns the app and the code?

You do. The code, the accounts, the domain and the App Store listing are set up in your name.

iPhone, Android or web?

We usually start with a web app or a prototype that works on every phone, then build native iPhone (and Android if needed) once the idea is proven.

Can you help with an idea in a regulated area?

Yes, we'll flag the rules early (payments, gambling, health, privacy) and design around them. We also recommend getting proper legal advice before launch.
